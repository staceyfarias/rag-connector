## IPS vs. GST

IPS passengers do not visit Ceres, Saturn, Titan, or Enceladus. The Moon Base 7 layover is transit-only (4 hours) with no shore excursion time — no golf, no Heritage tour, no rover. Passengers who want Moon surface excursions must book the Grand Solar Tour. The IPS return is direct from Venus to Earth; the GST continues to the outer system after Venus.

## Total EVA time (IPS)

Surface EVA is available on Mars only — up to three excursions over three days. Each excursion is approximately 4 to 6 hours of EVA time. No EVA is available on any other IPS leg.

# Glass Floor Tour — The Aphrodite, Venus

*Pelorus Voyages · Excursion brochure · 2042–43 season*

**Stand on 4 centimetres of glass. Below: 52 kilometres of Venusian atmosphere, cloud layers in gold and amber, and the glow of a surface at 465 degrees Celsius.**

The Glass Floor Tour is a 90-minute guided walk along the underside observation corridor of The Aphrodite. The floor is rated borosilicate glass, 4 centimetres thick, running the full length of the station's lower hull. The guide stops at four viewing stations where the panels are widest. Below you, the upper cloud layer churns in shades of gold, amber, and deep orange. Lightning flickers in the layers below. The faint red-orange glow at the bottom of the atmosphere is the surface. Most guests stop halfway across. Some lie flat on the glass.

No other experience in the solar system puts you this close to the Venusian cloud deck from inside a pressurised environment. The view through photography is compelling. The view through 4 centimetres of glass, standing on it, with nothing below your feet for 52 kilometres, is different in kind.

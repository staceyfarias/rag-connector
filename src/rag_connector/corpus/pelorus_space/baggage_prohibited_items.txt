The following items may not be brought aboard any Pelorus Voyages vessel or taken onto any surface excursion, regardless of cabin class. This list applies in addition to all standard international spaceflight safety regulations. Violations discovered at baggage screening result in confiscation; violations discovered aboard the vessel during flight may result in immediate disembarkation at the next stop at the passenger's expense.

Combustible and flammable materials are prohibited. This includes liquid-fuel lighters, strike-anywhere matches, aerosol cans with flammable propellants, and camping fuel canisters of any type. Battery-powered lighters with an enclosed heating element are permitted.

Unsealed biological samples are prohibited. This applies both to biological material brought from Earth and to any biological or geological material collected at a destination. Regolith, rocks, ice, and surface material from any stop may not be transported in the passenger cabin, in checked baggage, or on the passenger's person. This prohibition exists due to both contamination protocols and heritage preservation law. Sealed scientific samples collected under an approved research permit and transported in certified containment may be an exception — contact Pelorus Voyages scientific coordination in advance.

Self-powered drones and remotely piloted aircraft are prohibited aboard the vessel and at all stops except the Ceres Waystation surface zone, where licensed recreational drones under 500 grams are permitted with advance notification to the Waystation Hub. Drones are explicitly prohibited at all heritage sites including Tranquility Base, Fra Mauro, and the Cydonia Formation interpretive zone.

Firearms and projectile weapons of any type are prohibited. This includes licensed weapons, inert display pieces, and parts.

Undeclared radioactive materials are prohibited. Declared medical radioactive materials — such as radiopharmaceuticals for personal treatment — require advance approval from Pelorus Voyages medical operations and must be transported in certified shielded containers. Contact accessibility support at least 60 days before departure.

High-powered laser devices above 5 milliwatts output are prohibited. Standard laser pointers are permitted but may not be directed at any heritage site object, any vessel window, or any crew or passenger. Laser devices are prohibited at Tranquility Base and Fra Mauro specifically under heritage protection regulations.

Alcohol brought from Earth for personal consumption is limited to one litre per passenger. The vessel's bar service is the intended source for in-voyage alcohol consumption.

Pelorus Voyages accepts the following payment methods for all voyage bookings, add-on purchases, and excursion fees. Not all methods are available for all transaction types; restrictions are noted below.

Major credit and debit cards are accepted for all transaction types including deposits, balance payments, add-ons, and on-voyage purchases at the bar and excursion desks. Accepted card networks are Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Cards issued by non-Earth banking institutions must be registered in the account portal before use; some Mars Colonial Bank and Lunar Credit Union cards require a one-time identity verification step on first use.

Bank transfer is accepted for voyage deposits and balance payments only. Bank transfers are not accepted for on-voyage purchases. Pelorus Voyages supports SWIFT, IBAN, and the Interplanetary Clearing Network (ICN) for non-Earth accounts. Allow five to seven business days for bank transfer payments to clear; the booking is not confirmed until the transfer is received. Bank transfers via the ICN may take up to 10 business days depending on the relay schedule.

Orbital Credits are the Pelorus Voyages stored-value account. Credits can be loaded by credit card, bank transfer, or as a refund from a cancelled voyage. Orbital Credits are accepted for all transaction types including on-voyage purchases and can be used to pay any portion of a booking. Credits expire 24 months after the last transaction on the account if no qualifying voyage has taken place. Orbital Credits are distinct from Orbital Points — they are a payment method, not a loyalty currency.

Orbital Points may be redeemed toward voyage bookings and cabin class upgrades through the points redemption function in the account portal. Points redemption converts a points allocation into a voyage credit that offsets the payment amount. Points cannot be used directly as a payment method, cannot be redeemed for cash, and cannot be transferred to another passenger's account. Points redemption for upgrades must be completed at least seven days before departure; within seven days, upgrades must be paid by cash or card.

Corporate accounts are available for organisations booking five or more voyages per year. Corporate accounts receive consolidated invoicing, priority group booking access, and a dedicated account coordinator. Payment is via bank transfer on net-30 terms after invoice issue. Corporate accounts must be established before booking; retroactive conversion of individual bookings to a corporate account is not available.

Gift vouchers issued by Pelorus Voyages are redeemable as a payment method for voyage deposits and balance payments. Vouchers are single-use and do not have change returned; unused value above a single payment amount is forfeited unless the voucher specifies otherwise.

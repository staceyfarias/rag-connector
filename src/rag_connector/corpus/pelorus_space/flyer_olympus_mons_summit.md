# Olympus Mons Caldera Rim — Mars Colony 1

**The summit of the solar system's largest volcano. 21.9 kilometres above the Martian surface. An 80-kilometre-wide caldera at your feet.**

Olympus Mons rises higher than any mountain on Earth and spans a base larger than the state of Arizona. It has been dormant for tens of millions of years. The caldera at its summit is 80 kilometres in diameter — a collapse structure formed by successive eruption events that drained the magma chambers below. At the rim, you stand above an 80-kilometre void that is several kilometres deep.

The Caldera Rim excursion involves a pressurised shuttle flight from Mars Colony 1 to a base camp at 19 kilometres elevation, a guide-led traverse to the caldera rim at 21.9 kilometres, and a return to base camp for the flight back. Total duration approximately 10 hours. At the rim: an 80-kilometre-wide collapse complex. The scale does not resolve quickly. Most participants describe a period of sitting at the edge and simply waiting for what they are looking at to register.

**The Olympus Fitness Test** assesses cardiovascular and musculoskeletal capacity for sustained exertion in the EVA suit under Martian atmospheric conditions. It is conducted at Mars Colony 1 by Pelorus Voyages medical staff. It is pass/fail; failure disqualifies the passenger from the Caldera Rim excursion on that voyage. A passed result is valid only for the current voyage.

Pathfinder passengers should book the fitness test for their first morning at MC-1. Slots are limited and fill quickly at peak departure windows. The standard voyage medical clearance does not substitute for this test; passengers who arrive at the Caldera Rim departure gate without a passed fitness test result on file will be turned away.

The Caldera Rim hike is included in the Pathfinder base fare and does not require a separate add-on purchase. The Olympus Fitness Test is conducted at no additional charge.

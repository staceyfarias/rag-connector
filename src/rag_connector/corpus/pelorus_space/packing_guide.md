# Packing and Baggage Guide

*Pelorus Voyages · Passenger Services · Edition 5 · Issued 2 May 2042*

This guide covers your personal mass allowance, what you cannot bring, what equipment is available to rent at each stop, photography rules at heritage sites, and how to handle personal medications. Because everything on a spacecraft is weighed, mass management requires more planning than a typical holiday. Read this before you start packing.

## 1. Personal Mass Allowance

Mass is measured at the Earth launch terminal baggage drop and applies to everything you carry — checked bags, carry-on to your berth, and personal items in your pockets or worn on your body. Allowances are per passenger, not per cabin. Two Navigator passengers sharing a cabin each have their own 18 kg allowance; they cannot pool them.

| Cabin class | Personal mass allowance | Equipment rental credits | Additional mass rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pioneer (PCL)** | 12 kg | None | USD 180/kg |
| **Navigator (NCL)** | 18 kg | 1× rental credit per voyage | USD 150/kg |
| **Pathfinder (PFL)** | 25 kg | 2× rental credits per voyage | USD 120/kg |

**Equipment rental credits** entitle the passenger to one rental of any single equipment item at any stop where rental is available. Standard rental items include EVA equipment upgrades, photography equipment, golf club sets at the Lunar Golf Links, and Ceres surface sports equipment. Credits are redeemed at the excursion check-in desk. Unused credits do not carry over to future voyages and have no cash value.

**Overweight baggage fees** are assessed at the Earth launch terminal only. Individual stops do not re-weigh passengers' belongings. Fees are charged before boarding and must be settled before the passenger proceeds to Gate 22.

**Personal golf clubs** — a standard six-club set with bag weighs approximately 4.5 kg and counts against personal mass allowance with no exemption. Clubs must be presented to the Moon Base 7 equipment desk at least 2 hours before the excursion departure for pressurisation assessment. Transit from Moon Base 7 to the Fra Mauro excursion site is provided at no additional charge once cleared.

**Camera and photography equipment** — camera bodies, lenses, and accessories count against personal mass allowance. A full interchangeable-lens kit can easily represent 3 to 5 kg of a Pioneer passenger's 12 kg limit. Passengers planning extensive photography should factor equipment weight into their packing decision or consider a cabin class upgrade for additional allowance.

**Nothing is exempt** from the mass allowance except personal medications carried in original pharmacy containers with a supporting prescription or physician letter. CPAP machines, portable oxygen, injectable medication cooling cases, camera equipment, and all clothing count against the allowance. Passengers whose medical equipment would take them meaningfully over their class allowance should contact accessibility support before booking.

## 2. What Cannot Be Brought Aboard

The following items are prohibited on all Pelorus Voyages vessels and surface excursions, regardless of cabin class. Items discovered at baggage screening are confiscated without exception. Items discovered aboard during flight may result in disembarkation at the next stop at the passenger's expense.

**Combustible and flammable materials.** Liquid-fuel lighters, strike-anywhere matches, aerosol cans with flammable propellants, and camping fuel canisters of any type. Battery-powered lighters with an enclosed heating element are permitted.

**Unsealed biological samples.** This applies to biological material from Earth and to any material collected at a destination. Regolith, rocks, ice, and surface material from any stop cannot be transported in the passenger cabin, in checked baggage, or on the passenger's person. The prohibition covers both contamination protocol and heritage preservation law. Sealed scientific samples under an approved research permit in certified containment may be an exception — contact Pelorus Voyages scientific coordination at least 60 days before departure.

**Self-powered drones and remotely piloted aircraft.** Prohibited aboard the vessel and at all stops except the Ceres Waystation surface zone, where recreational drones under 500 grams are permitted with advance notification to the Waystation Hub. Drones are explicitly prohibited at all heritage sites: Tranquility Base, Fra Mauro, and the Cydonia Formation interpretive zone.

**Firearms and projectile weapons.** Includes licensed weapons, inert display pieces, and component parts.

**Undeclared radioactive materials.** Declared medical radioactive materials (radiopharmaceuticals for personal treatment) require advance approval from Pelorus Voyages medical operations and must be transported in certified shielded containers. Contact accessibility support at least 60 days before departure.

**High-powered laser devices above 5 milliwatts.** Standard laser pointers are permitted but may not be directed at heritage site objects, vessel windows, or any crew or passengers. All laser devices are prohibited at Tranquility Base and Fra Mauro under heritage protection regulations.

**Alcohol above 1 litre per passenger.** The vessel's bar service is the intended source for in-voyage alcohol consumption.

## 3. Equipment Rentals by Stop

Equipment rental is available at most stops and covers items passengers prefer not to carry in their personal mass allowance. Navigator passengers receive 1 rental credit per voyage; Pathfinder passengers receive 2. Pioneer passengers may rent at standard rates without a credit. Unused credits expire at voyage end.

**Moon Base 7 / Lunar Golf Links** — Golf club rental sets include six clubs and two balls, calibrated for the low-gravity lunar swing arc. The rental set is appropriate for all skill levels. Personal clubs from Earth offer no practical advantage for most passengers and consume 4.5 kg of allowance.

**Mars Colony 1** — Limited surface excursion equipment beyond the standard EVA suit. Specialist photography equipment (additional lighting rigs, stabilisation mounts for long exposures) available at the MC-1 expedition outfitter; rental credit applies. Dust-resistance boots for the Cydonia fine regolith available as a low-cost add-on without a credit.

**Venus — The Aphrodite** — Pool deck equipment (paddleboards, aqua volleyball sets, snorkelling kits) is included in pool access and does not require a rental credit. Photography equipment including underwater housings for pool photography is available for rent; credit applies.

**Ceres Waystation** — Low-gravity traction boots (recommended for the Occator crater rim scramble), photography mounting rigs for wide-angle surface shots, and the Leap harness system. The Leap harness is included in The Leap booking, not a separate rental item.

**Titan Ice Flats** — Cold suits are provided and mandatory; they are not rented but are part of the excursion package. Ice sports equipment (skates, sleds, long-jump platform equipment) is provided by the excursion operator at Excursion Base Alpha and included in the excursion price. No rental credit is consumed by Titan excursion equipment. Personal cold-weather sports gear from Earth is not permitted on the Titan surface due to cold-suit certification requirements.

**Saturn Ring Transit / Enceladus** — No equipment rental available. Both are passive observation stops with no surface access.

## 4. Cameras and Photography at Heritage Sites

Photography is encouraged at all Pelorus Voyages destinations. Most stops have no restrictions. The heritage sites have specific rules enforced under interplanetary law; violations are reported by the tour guide and carry serious consequences.

**Tranquility Base Heritage Site** — governed by the Outer Space Heritage Preservation Act of 2041 (OSHPA 2041) and the Lunar Heritage Authority access agreement.
- Photography from the marked perimeter: permitted without restriction
- Flash photography: prohibited within 50 metres of the heritage boundary flagging
- Drones or remotely piloted aircraft: prohibited within the entire heritage zone (2 kilometres beyond the visible perimeter)
- Laser pointers or laser range-finding equipment: prohibited — may not be directed at the flag, the Lunar Module descent stage, or any other heritage object

**Fra Mauro zone (Lunar Golf Links area)**
- Drones: prohibited throughout the zone, including the 500-metre protection radius around the Shepard Shot Marker
- Physical approach within 20 metres of the Shepard Shot Marker prohibited
- Photography from beyond 20 metres is permitted without restriction

**Cydonia Formation complex (Mars)**
- Photography from designated viewing areas: permitted
- Drones of any size: prohibited throughout the Cydonia Formation interpretive zone and the Category B scientific preservation zone — drones are permitted only in the Ceres Waystation surface zone (see Section 2)
- Professional photography and scientific equipment: requires a research permit from the Mars Surface Heritage Authority

**Consequences of violations** — violations at Tranquility Base or Fra Mauro are reported immediately to the Lunar Heritage Authority by the tour guide. Pelorus Voyages is legally required to report violations and does not have discretion to decline reporting. Consequences range from permanent exclusion from heritage sites to criminal prosecution under OSHPA 2041, which carries fines and in serious cases custodial sentences.

## 5. Personal Medications

Medications for personal use are handled differently from other baggage items.

**Mass allowance exemption** — prescription and over-the-counter medications in original pharmacy containers, supported by a current prescription or physician letter, are exempt from the personal mass allowance. The medications themselves are mass-exempt; the case, cooling unit, or bag used to carry them counts toward the allowance. Pelorus Voyages recommends carrying medication cases that weigh no more than 1 kg when empty.

**Temperature-controlled medications** — biologics, insulin, and some liquid antibiotics requiring refrigeration may be transported in a personal insulated medication case (mass-exempt for the medications; the case counts toward allowance). The vessel medical bay can provide supplementary temperature-controlled storage for passengers whose requirements exceed what a personal case provides — contact accessibility support before departure to arrange this.

**Declaration** — all medications must be declared in the medical section of the account portal before departure. The declaration does not need dosing details but must list each medication by name (brand and generic), the condition it treats, and whether it requires refrigeration. This information is shared with the vessel's flight surgeon before departure to ensure medical bay inventory compatibility.

**Controlled substances** — medications scheduled under the laws of the passenger's country of origin must be accompanied by the original prescription. Additional transit documentation may be required at interplanetary waypoints; requirements vary by waypoint authority. Moon Base 7 and Ceres Waystation customs desks publish current controlled substance declaration requirements on their official information portals. Pelorus Voyages cannot advise on third-party waypoint requirements.

**Prohibited medications** — certain medications cannot be brought aboard due to vessel safety rules:
- Aerosol inhalers using flammable propellants (dry powder inhalers and soft mist inhalers are acceptable alternatives)
- Liquid oxygen concentrators (solid-state supplemental oxygen is available from the medical bay)
- Injectable controlled substances unless declared in advance and transported in certified sealed containers

Passengers who take a medication on the prohibited list should contact Pelorus Voyages medical operations at least 60 days before departure to discuss alternatives. In most cases a therapeutic equivalent compatible with vessel regulations exists.

**Carry-on, with a buffer** — pack essential medications in your carry-on, not checked baggage, and carry a 14-day supply beyond the voyage duration. This covers the possibility of a scrub, diversion, or leg extension. Medications in checked baggage separated from the passenger in a baggage incident are not replaceable mid-voyage.

**Name discrepancies** — if the medication name on the pharmacy label differs from what appears in your medical documents (brand vs. generic, international naming conventions), carry a physician letter clarifying the equivalence. Customs authorities at interplanetary waypoints have varied pharmacopeias; original containers plus a physician letter are the most reliable documentation.

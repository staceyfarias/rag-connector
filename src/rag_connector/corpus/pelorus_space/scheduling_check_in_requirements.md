# Check-in and muster deadlines — Pelorus Voyages

All times are minutes **before the published departure window opening**. Missing the muster deadline closes the manifest. The vessel will not hold.

| Stop | Check-in deadline | Muster point | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth Launch Terminal | 180 min | Terminal 7, Gate 22 | Baggage drop must be completed 4 hours before departure. Biometric check-in opens 6 hours prior. |
| Moon Base 7 — IPS / ORT transit | 90 min | Bay 3 Airlock, Level B | All surface excursions must end and suits returned before entering Bay 3. Excursion equipment drop-off adds 15 min. |
| Moon Base 7 — GST extended layover | 90 min | Bay 3 Airlock, Level B | Golf Links last tee time must be booked no later than 2 hours before muster. Rover tour must return 2 hours before muster. |
| Mars Colony 1 | 60 min | MC-1 Airlock Bay B, Hab Ring 2 | All EVA suits must be returned and docked before entering Airlock Bay B. EVA suit return takes 10–15 min. Factor this into excursion end time. |
| Venus — The Aphrodite | 30 min | Docking Pier 1, Resort Deck 2 | Pool deck and sun deck close 45 min before departure. Glass floor tour final group must exit by 60 min before departure. Gondola must return by 90 min before departure (gondola recovery time). |
| Ceres Waystation | 45 min | Waystation Hub Central, Dock 4 | Surface tether lines must be returned and logged before entering the pressurised hub. Tether return at the equipment station adds 10 min. The Leap platform closes 60 min before departure. |

| Titan Ice Flats | 75 min | Excursion Base Alpha, Airlock 2 | Cold-suit decontamination is mandatory and takes approximately 20 min. Plan your excursion to end at least 95 min before the departure window. Ice sports equipment must be returned before decontamination begins. |
| Enceladus Geyser Flyover | N/A | N/A — no surface stop | Observation deck viewing only. No disembarkation at this stop. Plume residue cleaning of observation windows begins 60 min after closest approach and may restrict visibility. |

## Important notes

**The Titan cold-suit rule is the most commonly missed.** Passengers frequently plan to arrive at Excursion Base Alpha 75 minutes before departure, not accounting for the mandatory 20-minute decontamination. Effective minimum return time is 95 minutes before the departure window.

**Venus gondola recovery.** The gondola takes 30–40 minutes to dock and secure after descent. The gondola must begin its return ascent no later than 90 minutes before departure. Book accordingly — the gondola operator enforces this independently.

**Moon Base 7 golf.** Round times vary with party size and experience. Pelorus Voyages recommends booking the morning round (0700–1100 departure times) rather than the afternoon round to avoid close muster margins.

**Muster is not boarding.** Presenting at the muster point by the deadline means you are counted on the manifest. Boarding then proceeds in cabin class order. Passengers who arrive at muster with fewer than 20 minutes remaining before departure window opening may not complete boarding before the airlock seals.

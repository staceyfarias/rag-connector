# Medical clearance requirements by destination — Pelorus Voyages

Standard voyage medical clearance (issued by the passenger's physician, valid 90 days from sign-off) is required for all itineraries. Some destinations require additional clearance components beyond the standard. These must be arranged before departure from Earth; they cannot be obtained during the voyage.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Notes

**Olympus Fitness Test** is not the same as standard medical clearance. It assesses sustained exertion capacity at Mars atmospheric pressure levels and requires approximately two hours at MC-1. It is Pathfinder-only; no exceptions. Book the test at MC-1 on arrival, not in advance.

**Gondola cardiovascular clearance** specifically assesses the cardiovascular response to the pressure changes during the 3-km descent into the Venusian atmosphere. The pressure differential is not extreme but is detectable. Passengers with any history of cardiovascular events should discuss the gondola specifically — not just spaceflight in general — with their physician.

**Titan cold-suit certification and cardiovascular check** are both conducted at Moon Base 7 and typically take half a day. Passengers booking the ORT or GST who intend to do the Titan surface excursion should factor this appointment into their Moon Base 7 planning on arrival. Both must be completed before departure for Ceres.

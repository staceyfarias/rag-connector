# Family Travel: Age Limits at a Glance

*Pelorus Voyages · Family travel brochure · 2042–43 season*

**Bring the whole family to the edge of the solar system. Here is everything you need to know about who can do what, and when.**

Pelorus Voyages welcomes travellers from age 5 on every itinerary. A child's first sight of Earth shrinking behind the observation deck, a first low-gravity bounce at Mars Colony 1, a first look down through the clouds of Venus: these are moments families talk about for decades. To keep every excursion safe and every young explorer comfortable, some activities have a minimum age. This page gathers them in one place so you can plan your family's voyage with confidence.

**The voyage itself**

Children must be at least 5 years old to travel on any Pelorus Voyages itinerary, and children under 5 cannot travel under any circumstances. Every passenger under 18 must travel with a parent or guardian as a co-passenger.

**What "firm" means**

No minimum age can be waived, and the Glass Floor Tour (8), the Gondola Descent (16) and The Leap (16) are firm limits: parental consent, a signed waiver or any other arrangement cannot override them. Age is measured on the day of the excursion, not the day you book, so a birthday during the voyage can open a new adventure. Bring identification for every young traveller to each excursion check-in.

**Waivers and parents at check-in**

Most active excursions require a signed waiver. For any passenger under 18, a parent or guardian must be present at excursion check-in to co-sign it. Children aged 5 to 11 stay with a parent or guardian at all times on surface excursions. Children aged 12 and over may join eligible excursions without a parent alongside, once a parent has co-signed the waiver at check-in.

**Avoid a disappointment at the gate**

A child below an excursion's minimum age will be turned away at check-in, and the excursion fee is not refunded. The one exception is a booking that our booking system accepted when it should have refused it; if you believe that happened, contact support.

**Planning tips for families**

- Book age-limited excursions early. Popular tours such as the Glass Floor fill weeks in advance.
- Teenagers heading for Titan need their cold-suit certification and cardiovascular check at Moon Base 7 before the vessel leaves for the outer system.
- For The Leap, sign the waiver at Moon Base 7; it cannot be signed at Ceres.

Full details, including medical requirements for each activity, are in Section 6 of the Medical Clearance and Pre-Voyage Fitness Guide. We can't wait to welcome your family aboard.

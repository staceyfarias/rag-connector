# Check-in and Muster Deadlines

*Pelorus Voyages · Voyage Operations · Card VO-12 · Rev. 3, June 2042*

**Keep this card with your boarding documents.**

All times are minutes **before the published departure window opening**. Missing the muster deadline closes the manifest. The vessel will not hold.

## Remember

**Titan: 95, not 75.** The posted muster is 75 minutes. Decontamination takes 20 more. Your real return time from the surface is 95 minutes before the departure window.

**Ceres: The Leap closes first.** The platform closes 60 minutes before the departure window — 15 minutes ahead of the 45-minute general muster. Finish The Leap by 60.

**Venus gondola.** Docking and securing take 30 to 40 minutes after the ascent. The operator enforces the 90-minute cutoff independently and will not extend it.

**Moon Base 7 golf.** Book a morning round (starting 0700–1100) on the GST layover. Afternoon rounds leave thin muster margins.

**Build in a buffer.** Aim to be back 30 minutes before the muster deadline, not at it.

**Muster is not boarding.** Reaching the muster point on time puts you on the manifest. Boarding then follows in order. Arrive with fewer than 20 minutes left before the departure window and you may not board before the airlock seals.

## If you miss the muster

The vessel departs without you. Go straight to the Pelorus Voyages expedition coordinator at the stop. Coordinator posts stay staffed for four hours after each departure.

## Family travel notes

Children aged 5 to 11 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at all times on surface excursions. Children aged 12 and above may participate in eligible excursions without a parent present, provided a parent or guardian has co-signed the excursion waiver at check-in.

No minimum age is waivable. Age limits marked as firm cannot be overridden by parental consent, signed waiver, or any other mechanism. Passengers who present at an excursion with a minor below the firm minimum age will be turned away; the excursion fee will not be refunded unless the age restriction was clearly misrepresented in the booking system (contact support if you believe this occurred).

# Personal mass allowance — Pelorus Voyages

Mass allowances are per passenger, not per cabin. All allowances are measured at the Earth launch terminal baggage drop and include the weight of the luggage itself. Carry-on items taken to the berth count toward the allowance; nothing is exempt.

| Cabin class | Personal mass allowance | Equipment rental credits included | Camera gear | Additional mass (per kg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pioneer (PCL)** | 12 kg | None | Included in 12 kg allowance | USD 180/kg |
| **Navigator (NCL)** | 18 kg | 1× rental credit per voyage | Included in 18 kg allowance | USD 150/kg |
| **Pathfinder (PFL)** | 25 kg | 2× rental credits per voyage | Included in 25 kg allowance | USD 120/kg |

## Equipment rental credits

A rental credit entitles the passenger to one rental of any single equipment item at any stop where rental is available. Standard rental items include: cold-suit gear (Titan only — though cold suits are mandatory and provided, a rental credit can offset the standard excursion surcharge), EVA equipment upgrades, photography equipment, golf club sets at the Lunar Golf Links, and Ceres surface sports equipment. Rental credits are redeemed at the excursion check-in desk at each stop. Unused credits do not carry over to future voyages.

## Personal golf clubs

Personal golf clubs declared and cleared at Moon Base 7 are transported to the Fra Mauro excursion site at no additional charge and count against the passenger's personal mass allowance. A standard six-club set with bag weighs approximately 4.5 kg. Clubs must be presented to the Moon Base 7 equipment desk at least 2 hours before the excursion departure for pressurisation assessment.

## Prohibited from mass allowance exemptions

All of the following count toward personal mass allowance with no exemption: medical equipment (CPAP machines, portable oxygen, injectable medications with cooling cases), camera bodies and lenses, personal golf clubs, and clothing. Passengers requiring medical equipment that would take them meaningfully over their allowance should contact accessibility support before booking to discuss options.

## Baggage measurement

Baggage is measured at the Earth launch terminal only. Individual stops do not re-weigh passengers' belongings. Overweight baggage fees assessed at Earth launch apply to the full voyage and are charged before boarding.
